# VisioFix privacy policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

**Short version:** VisioFix works entirely on your computer. It doesn't collect, send, sell or share
anything about you or the pages you visit. There are no analytics, no accounts, no ads and no remote
code.

## What VisioFix reads, and why

Everything below happens inside your browser, on your computer. None of it is sent anywhere.

- **The pages you visit (website content).** To repair a page, VisioFix looks for buttons, links,
  form fields and images that screen readers can't name, and adds what's missing (for example,
  connecting a form field to its label). When you open Focus Mode, it reads the page's text to build
  the reading view, and read-aloud reads that text to you. VisioFix doesn't store page content: the
  repairs live in the page itself and disappear when you leave it, and Focus Mode's copy is thrown
  away when you close it.
- **Your settings.** Saved with the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on
  your computer only, not synced to any account: whether repairs are on, the sites where you paused
  repairs (their addresses, such as `example.com`), Focus Mode's theme and text size, the read-aloud
  voice and speed, and whether online voices are allowed. Removing VisioFix deletes them.

## Read-aloud and online voices

Read-aloud uses your browser's built-in speech. By default VisioFix only uses voices installed on your
computer, which work without the internet.

Some browsers also offer online voices, such as Chrome's "Google …" voices and Edge's "… Online
(Natural)" voices. VisioFix only uses them if you turn on **Allow online voices** in its settings
(off by default). When an online voice reads, your browser sends the text being read to the
company that runs the voice (Google or Microsoft), under that company's privacy policy. VisioFix
itself never receives or keeps that text.

## What VisioFix never does

- It never sends data to its developer or anyone else. It makes no network requests of its own: its
  code, font and icons are part of the extension. (Focus Mode shows the page's own pictures, which
  your browser loads from that website, just as the page would.)
- No analytics, tracking, crash reports or advertising.
- No selling or transferring of data, no use of data for anything other than the features above,
  and no use of data to decide creditworthiness or for lending.
- Nobody reads your data, because none of it leaves your computer.

The use of information received by VisioFix complies with the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Permissions

- **Storage:** saves your settings on your computer.
- **Read and change data on the websites you visit:** needed to repair pages automatically and to
  open Focus Mode on any site. VisioFix only adds missing labels; it never deletes page content, and
  turning it off for a site puts that site's pages back exactly as they were.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
